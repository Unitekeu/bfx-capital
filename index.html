Questions fréquentes

Tout ce qu'il faut savoir avant de connecter votre compte.

How does Manual Copy differ from Automatic Copy?

In Manual mode, every trade instruction is sent to your account but requires your confirmation before execution. In Automatic mode, the client EA executes authorized trades instantly, within your configured risk limits. You can switch between modes or disable copying at any time.

What happens when my account hits the 3% daily loss limit?

New trades and automatic reversals are blocked immediately, while existing protective stop losses stay in place. You receive an email alert, and the account remains blocked until the next trading day.

Are my MT5 credentials stored on the platform?

No. Communication between your MT5 account and our backend uses secure, revocable authentication tokens, never plain-text credentials. You can revoke access instantly from your dashboard.

What lot sizes and risk levels are supported?

Lot sizes range from a minimum of 0.003 to a maximum of 0.14 per position, validated against your broker’s specifications. Risk per trade is configurable between 0.5% and 2%, enforced independently for every connected account.

Does Auto-Reverse trade on every opposite signal?

No. Auto-Reverse only triggers after confirmation of a genuine opposite trading signal. The robot closes the existing position, re-verifies execution and risk availability, then opens the reverse position — never on a single contrarian tick.
